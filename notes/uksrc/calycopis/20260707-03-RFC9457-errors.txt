    Stop Returning {success:false}: RFC 9457 Problem Details for Modern APIs
    https://www.learnhubly.com/blog/rfc-9457-problem-details-api-error-responses

        Status codes are necessary. They are not sufficient.
        A 422 Unprocessable Entity tells the client that validation failed.
        It does not say which field failed, why it failed, or what to change.
        A 409 Conflict signals a state collision − but not whether the conflict
        is a duplicate email, an optimistic lock version mismatch, or a business
        rule violation requiring a completely different resolution path.
